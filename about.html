About Atelier Brown

A men’s style advisor made for brown skin and Indian weather.

Where the advice comes from

The colour, fabric and fit rules follow The Brown Skin Style Guide, extended with wider menswear knowledge: collars, trouser cuts, patterns, layers and festive wear.

How it decides

  • Size comes from your height and weight, as an estimate. Always try before you buy.
  • Colours follow your undertone: warm skin leans terracotta and navy, neutral leans teal.
  • Fabrics follow season and occasion, from linen in heat to quick-dry in monsoon.

Your privacy

Photos for skin-tone detection are read in your browser and never uploaded. Your choices are saved only on your own device.

Honest limits

Colour from a photo depends on light and camera, and undertone is a rough guess. Treat the 3D model as a styled preview, not a scan of you.
